Portfolio of Augusto Gandia

(November 2025 - Mendoza, Argentina)


My work explores the relation between form and force. This last one is defined by time and movement, which relies on digital means to make people move in new ways (physically and cognitively). This new latent object is referred to as an Active Inert and as Ulmer's comment suggests it enacts a requalification of creative praxis from static to dynamic environments, possibly outer space. In Derrida's terms: ‘to comprehend the structure of a becoming, the form of a force, is to lose meaning by finding it’.
